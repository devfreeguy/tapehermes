tapehermes demo

This page lives on X Layer. It calls two outside services, and no API key is in any file you downloaded to see it.

Sites that use TapeHermes make outside calls, so they do not carry the “100% on-chain” badge.

1. Your wallet

Only the paid route needs a wallet. Signing a payment is free; the payment itself is a small amount in US dollars, shown before you confirm.

2. Free route: look up an asteroid

The site asks NASA on your behalf. The NASA key is kept by TapeHermes, never sent to you.

3. Paid route: look up a food

The site asks the US food database on your behalf and returns the best match. The key is kept by TapeHermes, and the number of results is fixed by the site owner, so you cannot ask for more. Price: shown from the manifest.

Food data: US Department of Agriculture, FoodData Central.

Paid route: a short answer

One short AI answer. The model, the instructions and the length are fixed by the site owner, and you only write the question. Price: shown from the manifest.

4. Check the answer yourself

Every answer is signed by the site. This list is what your browser checked before showing it.

Where the key is

  • The files of this site are on the chain. They hold no key, only this page and the helper that checks answers.
  • The keys are with TapeHermes, encrypted, and used only on the server for the two routes above.
  • Your answer is signed. If anyone changes it on the way, the check above fails.
