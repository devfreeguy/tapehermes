Paid APIs for TapeOut sites

Keep your API keys private, set a price for each call, and get paid in USDT on X Layer. You run no server.

Try it live How it works

Sites that use TapeHermes make outside calls, so they do not carry the “100% on-chain” badge.

How it works

  1. Add your key

    Save the API key of any service you already use. It is stored encrypted and never placed on the chain.

  2. Set a price

    Pick the action, the price per call and the limits. Your visitors cannot change what you fixed.

  3. Get paid per call

    A visitor signs a small USDT payment, gets the answer, and the money goes straight to your wallet.

Try it live

Two real actions run from this page. One is free and one costs a fraction of a cent.

1. Your wallet

Only the paid action needs a wallet. Signing a payment is free; the payment itself is a small amount in US dollars, shown before you confirm.

2. Free: look up an asteroid

The site asks NASA for you. The NASA key is kept by TapeHermes and never sent to you.

3. Paid: look up a food

The site asks the US food database for you and returns the best match. The key is kept by TapeHermes, and the number of results is fixed by the owner. Price: shown from the manifest.

Food data: US Department of Agriculture, FoodData Central.

Paid: a short answer

One short AI answer. The model, the instructions and the length are fixed by the owner, and you only write the question. Price: shown from the manifest.

Where the key is

  • The files of this site are on the chain. They hold no key, only this page and the helper that checks answers.
  • The keys are with TapeHermes, encrypted, and used only on the server for the two actions above.
  • Your answer is signed. If anyone changes it on the way, the check above fails.

Make one for your own site

You need a TapeOut site on X Layer, a wallet that holds it, and the API keys you want to protect.

  1. Sign in at dashboard.tapehermes.xyz with the wallet that holds your site.
  2. Add your keys, create your actions and set their prices.
  3. Publish your API list on X Layer with one signature.
